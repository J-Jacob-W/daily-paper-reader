## 摘要
目的：基于生态有效且客观的言语处理测量可以补充传统的听力学评估。音位相关电位（PRPs）是通过平均听者脑电图（EEG）对连续言语中音位的时间锁定反应推导得出的，已成为捕捉自然聆听条件下言语皮层处理的一种有前景的方法。重要的是，即使在常规听力图临床正常的情况下，PRPs也能揭示言语感知困难，这使得它们成为标准听力测量常遗漏的超阈值聆听困难的有希望的神经标志物。作为向临床转化迈出的关键一步，本研究考察了PRP衍生测量在与临床实施相关的现实世界情境（包括单耳与双耳呈现、刺激强度水平和重复测试时段）中的稳定程度。研究还评估了皮层对低层言语声学的追踪，以确定PRP发现是否可归因于声学处理。设计：记录了18名听力正常的年轻成年人在单耳或双耳以60 dB或75 dB聆听有声书言语时的EEG，间隔约一周进行两次会话。使用两个指标量化PRP中音位发音方式类别（元音、鼻音/近音、擦音和塞音）的神经分化：反映方式间变异相对于方式内变异的F统计量，以及从PRP训练预测方式类别的机器学习模型的分类准确率。时间响应函数建模评估了对连续声学包络和有声书言语起始特征的神经追踪。结果：PRP衍生的方式分化测量均未显示出会话、呈现方式、强度水平或其交互作用的显著影响。组内相关分析进一步表明这三个因素均具有中等至良好的可靠性。相反，双耳呈现下对声学包络和声学起始的神经追踪强于单耳呈现，双耳呈现诱发了更明显的皮层包络反应。结论：PRP衍生测量在临床测试环境中常见的适度程序变化下保持相对稳定，使PRP成为自然言语处理的有力客观指标。这种稳定性可能反映了对抽象、语言相关的言语类别的皮层处理，并表明PRP提供了超越外周听觉功能听力学评估和主要捕捉低层声学处理的EEG测量的补充信息。

## Abstract
Objectives: Objective and ecologically valid measures of speech processing can complement conventional audiologic assessments. Phoneme-related potentials (PRPs), derived by averaging listeners' electroencephalography (EEG) responses time-locked to phonemes in continuous speech, have emerged as a promising approach for capturing cortical processing of speech in naturalistic listening conditions. Importantly, PRPs reveal speech perception challenges even when conventional audiograms are clinically normal, positioning them as a promising neural marker for suprathreshold listening difficulties that standard audiometry often misses. As a critical step toward clinical translation, this study examined the extent to which PRP-derived measures remain stable across real-world contexts relevant to clinical implementation, including monaural versus binaural presentation, stimulus intensity level, and repeated testing sessions. The study also assessed cortical tracking of lower-level speech acoustics to determine whether the PRP findings could be attributed to acoustic processing. Design: EEG was recorded from 18 young adults with normal hearing as they listened to audiobook speech presented monaurally or binaurally at 60 or 75 dB across two sessions separated by approximately one week. Neural differentiation of phoneme manner-of-articulation classes (vowels, nasals/approximants, fricatives, and stops) in PRPs was quantified using two measures: an F-statistic reflecting between-manner relative to within-manner variability, and classification accuracy from a machine-learning model trained to predict manner class from PRPs. Temporal response function modeling assessed neural tracking of continuous acoustic envelope and onset features of the audiobook speech. Results: Neither PRP-derived measure of manner differentiation showed significant effects of session, presentation modality, intensity level, or their interactions. Intraclass correlation analyses further indicated moderate-to-good reliability across all three factors. In contrast, neural tracking of the acoustic envelope and acoustic onsets was stronger under binaural than monaural presentation, with binaural presentation eliciting more pronounced cortical responses to the envelope. Conclusions: PRP-derived measures remained relatively stable across modest procedural variations that are common in clinical testing contexts, positioning PRPs as a potent objective index of naturalistic speech processing. This stability may reflect cortical processing of abstract, linguistically relevant speech categories and suggest that PRPs provide complementary information beyond audiologic assessments of peripheral auditory functions and EEG measures that primarily capture lower-level acoustic processing.
