## 摘要
背景：试图表征冥想神经特征的脑电图研究通常依赖于与被动休息的对比或基于经验的实践者之间的比较。然而，这些方法很少包含主动控制状态，也很少建立所识别的定量脑电图特征的信度和稳健性。因此，所提出的冥想状态神经生理学标志的有效性仍不确定。本研究通过采用基于信度的、多会话被试内设计来克服这些局限性，以表征来自Brahmakumaris Rajayoga传统的有经验冥想者中独特的状态依赖脑电图动力学。方法：三十名长期冥想者在两天内进行了重复的脑电图记录，每天两次冥想训练。每个冥想块前后有休息期，两次训练之间有一个认知任务以减少携带效应。我们量化了宽带频谱功率、非周期性斜率和截距以及非线性动力学测量，包括去趋势波动分析、Higuchi分形维数和排列熵，在冥想、休息和任务条件下。结果：与休息和任务状态相比，冥想与增加的θ-α功率、升高的非周期性截距以及非线性指数（DFA、Higuchi、PE）的系统性调节相关。进一步，冥想核心特征表现出高会话间重测信度、强个体间一致性、在引导和静默冥想状态下的稳定性，并且不受冥想经验年限的调节。结论：本框架识别出一个可复现的冥想神经动力学核心，与被动和主动控制状态不同，涵盖了长期冥想者脑电图频谱、非周期性和非线性域。这些发现增强了冥想特异性神经标志的构建有效性和测量信度。

## Abstract
Background: Electroencephalographic (EEG) studies attempting to characterise the neural signature of meditation typically rely on contrasts with passive rest or comparisons among practitioners based on experience. However, these approaches rarely include active control states and seldom establish the reliability and robustness of identified quantitative EEG features. Consequently, the validity of proposed neurophysiological markers of meditative state remains uncertain. The present study addressed these limitations by using a reliability-informed, multi-session within-subject design to characterise distinct state-dependent EEG dynamics in experienced meditators from the Brahmakumaris Rajayoga tradition. Methods: Thirty long-term meditators underwent repeated EEG recordings over two days, comprising two meditation sessions per day. Each meditation block was flanked by rest periods, with a cognitive task between sessions to reduce carryover effects. We quantified broadband spectral power, aperiodic slope and intercept, and nonlinear dynamical measures, including detrended fluctuation analysis (DFA), Higuchi fractal dimension, and permutation entropy (PE), across meditation, rest, and task conditions. Results: Compared with both rest and task states, meditation was associated with increased theta-alpha power, an elevated aperiodic intercept, and systematic modulation of nonlinear indices (DFA, Higuchi, PE). Further meditative core features demonstrated high inter-session test-retest reliability, strong inter-individual consistency, stability across guided and silent meditation states, and were not moderated by years of meditative experience. Conclusion: The present framework identifies a reproducible neurodynamic core of meditation, distinct from passive and active control states, spanning spectral, aperiodic, and nonlinear EEG domains in long-term meditators. These findings enhance the construct validity and measurement reliability of meditation-specific neural markers.
